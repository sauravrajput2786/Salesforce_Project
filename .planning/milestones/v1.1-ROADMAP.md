## Overview

Milestone v1.1 implements enterprise Milestone Lifecycle and SLA Governance for Salesforce Service Cloud. It delivers end-to-end automated Service Cloud ticket lifecycle ensuring 100% SLA compliance, zero-touch milestone completion (resolving BUG-001), proactive escalation prevention, data immutability, customer CSAT survey triggers, and executive KPI visibility without manual agent overhead.

## Milestone Summary

**Key Architecture Decisions:**
- Realigned all 3 Entitlement warning triggers to exactly 75% elapsed duration (-15m, -60m, -120m).
- Protected Critical tickets against downgrade via before-save Flow `Case_Critical_Priority_Guard`.
- Single-Admin escalation routing in `Case_Escalation_Flow` accommodating Dev Edition org constraints without role hierarchy errors.
- Resolved BUG-001 by implementing `MilestoneCompletionService` and triggers on `EmailMessage` (outbound) and `Case` (close).
- Enterprise Data Governance via `VR_Case_Lock_Customer_And_SLA_Data` with `Bypass_Case_Lock` Custom Permission.
- Hybrid immutability allowing initial survey ingestion while permanently defending customer CSAT ratings from agent tampering.
- Executive Support KPI Dashboard delivering real-time visibility into CSAT ratings, SLA compliance, case volume, and resolution duration.
