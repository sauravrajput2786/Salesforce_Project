## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v1.1 implements enterprise Milestone Lifecycle and SLA Governance for Salesforce Service Cloud. The milestone achieved 100% of its planned objectives across 5 execution phases:
1. **SLA Entitlement Warning Synchronization:** Realigned warning triggers across Gold, Silver, and Platinum entitlement processes to fire at exactly 75% elapsed milestone time without premature priority elevation; protected Critical priority tickets from downgrade.
2. **Single-Admin Escalation Engine Governance:** Reconfigured scheduled escalations (24h/48h/72h) to dynamically target the active System Administrator, replaced noisy intermediate emails with in-app Chatter posts, and stamped full escalation telemetry at 72h.
3. **Milestone Auto-Completion Engine (Fix BUG-001):** Resolved BUG-001 by automating First Response milestone completion upon outbound customer emails and completing all remaining open milestones upon Case closure.
4. **SLA Breach Auditing & Data Governance:** Packaged standard workflow field updates for 100% milestone violations and enforced strict data immutability over intake data and SLA breach metrics via `VR_Case_Lock_Customer_And_SLA_Data` with `Bypass_Case_Lock` Custom Permission gating.
5. **CSAT Survey Automation & Executive Support KPI Dashboard:** Implemented automated CSAT survey triggering on Case closure with idempotency guards, synchronized 1–5 Star ratings and feedback comments with hybrid immutability protection, and built the Executive Support KPI Dashboard in ABC Service Console.
