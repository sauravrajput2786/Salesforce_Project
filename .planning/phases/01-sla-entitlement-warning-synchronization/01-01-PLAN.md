# Plan 01-01: SLA Warning Synchronization & Workflow Field Update Packaging

## Objective

Package the `ChangePriorityToHigh` workflow field update for Case and synchronize the proactive 75% elapsed warning time triggers across Gold (-15m), Silver (-60m), and Platinum (-120m) Entitlement Processes strictly on the "First Response to Customer" milestone, assigning priority elevation exclusively to 100% SLA violations.

## Context

- `01-CONTEXT.md` Decision D-01: 75% warning trigger applies strictly to "First Response to Customer" (Gold 60m -> -15m; Silver 240m -> -60m; Platinum 480m -> -120m).
- `01-CONTEXT.md` Decision D-02: At 75% warning, do NOT change Priority to High. It is strictly an internal proactive alert.
- `01-CONTEXT.md` Decision D-03: Priority elevation to High belongs strictly to 100% SLA Breach (Violation Action).
- Existing Entitlement Processes in `force-app/main/default/entitlementProcesses/` currently reference `<name>ChangePriorityToHigh</name><type>FieldUpdate</type>`, but `Case.workflow-meta.xml` does not yet exist in the repository.

- `.planning/phases/01-sla-entitlement-warning-synchronization/01-CONTEXT.md`
</read_first>

<action>
Update the 3 Entitlement Process XML files:
1. `Gold_Tier_SLA_Process.entitlementProcess-meta.xml`:
   - Under milestone `First Response to Customer` (60m):
     - Update warning `timeLength` to `-15` (45 min elapsed = 75%).
     - Move `<name>ChangePriorityToHigh</name>` from warning action to milestone violation action (`workflowTimeTriggerUnit: Minutes`, executed at breach).
2. `Silver_Tier_SLA_Process.entitlementProcess-meta.xml`:
   - Under milestone `First Response to Customer` (240m):
     - Update warning `timeLength` to `-60` (180 min elapsed = 75%).
     - Move `<name>ChangePriorityToHigh</name>` from warning action to milestone violation action.
3. `Platinum_Tier_SLA_Process.entitlementProcess-meta.xml`:
   - Under milestone `First Response to Customer` (480m):
     - Update warning `timeLength` to `-120` (360 min elapsed = 75%).
     - Move `<name>ChangePriorityToHigh</name>` from warning action to milestone violation action.
</action>

<acceptance_criteria>
- `Gold_Tier_SLA_Process.entitlementProcess-meta.xml` has `<timeLength>-15</timeLength>` under `First Response to Customer`.
