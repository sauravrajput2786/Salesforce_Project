## Objective

Create the end-to-end integration test suite ([Pillar4SlaGovernanceIntegrationTest.cls](file:///home/elsner/Salesforce/SalesforceProject/force-app/main/default/classes/Pillar4SlaGovernanceIntegrationTest.cls)) validating the complete Pillar 4 lifecycle: intake data immutability and SLA breach metric locking ([GOV-02](file:///home/elsner/Salesforce/SalesforceProject/.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md)), automated SLA breach tracking alignment ([GOV-01](file:///home/elsner/Salesforce/SalesforceProject/.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md)), Critical priority downgrade protection, outbound email first-response milestone completion, case closure milestone completion, and permitted non-locked field modifications.
